# Poster Studio Design Collection 2 Modern White Prompts

Last updated: July 30, 2026

These prompts are for generating the raster graphic layer before wiring the final assets into Poster Studio. They are designed for Stunity achievement posters with a fresh white-first international-school visual identity. The direction intentionally avoids repeating the older Khmer-ornamental, ribbon-heavy, ivory/gold poster language.

Do not copy any school logo, seal, partner badge, exact layout, text, or brand identity from the reference images. Use the references only to understand readability and academic-poster discipline. Do not reuse their red footer bar, golden ribbon sweeps, centered seal composition, or light-yellow/ivory tone.

## Shared Direction

Use this shared direction with every prompt unless a prompt says otherwise:

- Visual identity: modern Cambodian academic excellence, premium, clean, professional, inventive, easy to read.
- Base color: true clean white `#FFFFFF` as the dominant background. Very light neutral grey `#F5F7F8` may be used for depth only. Avoid yellow, cream, beige, tan, warm ivory, parchment, or aged paper.
- Accent palette: deep burgundy `#6B132B`, refined gold `#D8B35F`, deep academic teal `#0E5552`. Use accents sparingly, mostly as thin lines, small planes, luminous edges, or isolated geometric details.
- Cambodian detail: modern abstract Khmer kbach-inspired line systems, architectural contours, carved-stone rhythm translated into minimalist vector geometry, not traditional poster decoration.
- Landmark style: Angkor Wat, Bayon, Bakong, Banteay Srei, or Preah Vihear as contemporary line-art, translucent outline, embossed relief, or floating blueprint fragment. Never photorealistic, never central-heavy.
- Avoid: yellow background, ivory wash, beige paper, large ribbon waves, red footer bars, copied school poster composition, text, letters, numbers, logos, school names, faces, students, sample photos, watermarks, signatures, copied seals, partner badges, heavy confetti, crowded center, low-resolution artifacts.
- Output quality: high-end Photoshop and Illustrator finish, polished editorial design, print-ready, sharp edges, no pixelation.

When generating transparent PNG assets with a tool that cannot produce native alpha, generate on a perfectly flat chroma-key background and remove the background afterward. The final project asset must have true alpha transparency.

## 1. Square Background

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: square congratulation poster background for a web poster generator
Primary request: Create an original premium square achievement poster background using a new white-gallery Cambodian academic concept, not a ribbon poster.

Canvas:
- Exact 1:1 square composition
- 2160 x 2160 pixels
- High-resolution professional print and social-media quality

Core concept:
"White Gallery of Khmer Excellence" - a futuristic clean white exhibition surface where Cambodian landmark line-art, academic geometry, and tiny ceremonial accents float like curated gallery elements around the student area.

Creative direction:
Design a clean, sophisticated academic achievement background with the visual quality of a senior graphic designer working in Photoshop and Illustrator. The artwork must be original and must not copy any school poster, logo, layout, seal, footer, ribbon system, or brand identity.

Use a true white base covering at least 86% of the canvas. Keep the background visibly white, not ivory and not yellow. Add only cool pearl-grey shadows, white-on-white layered panels, soft glass-like translucent shapes, subtle architectural grid lines, micro-dot constellations, and faint embossed Khmer kbach contour lines.

Create visual interest through a new abstract system:
- floating thin gold contour lines like architectural measurement marks
- small burgundy and teal ceramic-glass tiles near the edges
- delicate circular orbit lines that suggest academic progress
- asymmetric white folded-paper planes with soft shadows
- minimal confetti points that look intentional and premium, not festive clutter

Add a contemporary Cambodian landmark feature as a low-contrast gallery watermark: Bayon faces or Angkor Wat drawn as fine blueprint-style line-art, placed diagonally from lower-left toward center-right but kept behind the white surface. Use pale grey, barely visible gold hairlines, and transparent teal shadows. It must feel like an elegant architectural drawing, not a tourist illustration.

Composition and safe zones:
- Keep the upper central 18% visually quiet for school logo, school name, and poster title
- Keep the central 58% clean for flexible student layouts: Top 3, Top 5, or Top 10
- Keep the bottom 12% calm for footer information
- Keep at least 7% clean white padding around all edges
- Decorative graphics must stay near the edges and behind the white layers; they must never dominate the student area
- The composition must remain balanced and recognizable at small social-media thumbnail size

Add restrained academic details near the edges only:
- abstract graduation-cap line icon built from thin gold and teal strokes
- tiny gold pinpoints, not heavy confetti
- geometric laurels broken into minimalist line fragments
- fine circular orbit geometry
- subtle Khmer kbach-inspired contour strokes

Lighting and finish:
Bright white studio lighting, cool clean shadows, subtle metallic-gold hairline highlights, crisp glass-paper layering, premium editorial polish, sharp print-ready detail.

Avoid:
No yellow tint, no ivory paper, no beige background, no large ribbon waves, no red footer bar, no copied school-poster layout, no text, no Khmer lettering, no English lettering, no numbers, no logos, no school emblems, no students, no faces, no photo frames, no watermarks, no signatures, no certificates, no large graduation objects, no heavy red background, no clutter in the central safe area.
```

## Suggested Generation Order

1. Generate the three backgrounds first: square, landscape, portrait A4.
2. Generate the three student frames with transparent output.
3. Generate the decorative elements pack.
4. Generate the landmark line-art pack.
5. Review assets at thumbnail size and full size before integrating into Poster Studio.

## Integration Notes

- Backgrounds should remain full-canvas raster assets.
- Student frames, decorative elements, and landmarks should be transparent PNGs or converted into optimized SVG where possible.
- Keep all generated text and student data in code, not inside the generated graphics.
- The same final graphic files should be used for both web preview and export to preserve exact visual parity.
