# Modern Khmer Excellence Future Background Prompts

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Reference style: `apps/web/public/poster-templates/modern-khmer-excellence/background-portrait-a4.png`

This prompt pack extends the Modern Khmer Excellence visual language into two future product directions:

- Large wall-print backgrounds for all teachers, full class structures, or group member boards with 50-100 people.
- Mobile full-screen wallpaper/profile-performance backgrounds for student self-selection, monthly leaderboards, quiz leaderboards, learning achievements, and social/profile surfaces in Stunity.

Use these prompts to generate background-only assets first. People, names, ranks, classes, scores, profile photos, badges, and leaderboard UI should be added later by the app.

## Shared Style

Use this visual identity for both prompts:

- Clean true white background with subtle white-on-white paper relief.
- Premium Khmer landmark relief and line-art: Angkor Wat, Bayon, Banteay Srei, Bakong, or Preah Vihear.
- Soft sculpted white cloud-like paper layers along edges or bottom.
- Very subtle Khmer kbach-inspired relief details, elegant and modern, not crowded.
- Fine gold architectural guide lines, constellation points, tiny academic markers, and thin circular geometry.
- Deep burgundy and deep teal corner fabric or enamel accents with thin gold trim, used sparingly.
- Premium Photoshop/Illustrator finish, high-end editorial design, crisp print quality.

Avoid:

- Text, numbers, letters, logos, school names, emblems, watermarks, signatures.
- People, faces, sample portraits, photo frames, certificates, medals, trophy objects.
- Crowded central areas, dark backgrounds, yellow/cream-heavy backgrounds, copied school poster layouts.
- Decorations that block future dynamic portraits, names, ranks, or leaderboard content.

## 1. Large Wall Poster Background - Teachers Or Class Structure

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: large-format wall poster background for a school staff board, teacher structure, class structure, or member board with 50 to 100 people
Primary request: Create an original premium large wall-print background in the Modern Khmer Excellence style for a Cambodian school group structure poster.

Canvas:
- Exact large-format print composition
- Recommended master size: 7200 x 4800 pixels, landscape 3:2
- 300 DPI-ready visual quality for large wall display
- Also adaptable to A1, A0, or custom school wall poster sizes

Creative direction:
Design a clean, prestigious, modern Cambodian academic background for displaying many people. The poster will later contain 50 to 100 teacher or student portraits arranged in a structured grid, so the background must be beautiful but very calm and readable.

Use a true white background covering at least 82% of the canvas. Add subtle white-on-white paper relief, soft sculpted Khmer-inspired cloud layers, elegant embossed kbach details near the edges, and low-contrast Cambodian landmark architecture along the bottom horizon. The artwork should feel like a premium school hall display, not a social media flyer.

Reference style:
Follow the mood of Modern Khmer Excellence: white relief depth, faint Khmer landmark carvings, thin gold guide lines, burgundy and teal corner accents, and refined gold trim. Create a new original composition, not a copy of the reference.

Composition:
- Keep the central 72% of the canvas extremely clean for a future grid of 50 to 100 portraits.
- Keep the top 12% calm for future school logo, school name, department title, and academic year.
- Keep the bottom 10% calm for future legend, footer, contact, or Stunity branding.
- Put decorative relief mostly along the outer frame, bottom horizon, and far corners.
- Avoid high-contrast elements behind the future portrait grid.
- Use a subtle inner margin and very thin gold alignment guide lines that can help the grid feel organized.

Visual structure:
- Bottom horizon: soft white relief silhouettes of Angkor Wat, Bayon, Banteay Srei, Bakong, or Preah Vihear, low opacity and integrated into sculpted white paper waves.
- Corners: restrained burgundy and deep teal folded-corner accents with thin gold trim.
- Side margins: delicate gold measurement ticks, small constellation nodes, academic line icons, and fine circular geometry.
- Background field: almost pure white with cool pearl-grey shadows and faint architectural blueprint hints.

Safe zones:
- Main grid safe area: from 12% to 82% vertical height, with at least 6% left/right padding.
- Header safe area: top 12%, quiet and mostly white.
- Footer safe area: bottom 10%, calm and lightly decorated.
- Decorative elements must never compete with small portrait labels.

Lighting and finish:
Bright clean studio lighting, soft paper-relief shadows, crisp gold hairline accents, premium editorial school display quality, elegant and trustworthy.

Avoid:
No text, no numbers, no logos, no school seals, no people, no sample portraits, no photo frames, no name labels, no dark or yellow background, no heavy red footer bar, no large decorative object in the center, no crowded confetti, no copied school branding.
```

## 2. Mobile Full-Screen Wallpaper Background - Leaderboard And Profile Performance

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: mobile full-screen wallpaper background for an e-learning and social app profile, student achievement, and leaderboard surface
Primary request: Create an original premium mobile wallpaper background in the Modern Khmer Excellence style for Stunity student profile performance and leaderboard experiences.

Canvas:
- Exact mobile full-screen portrait composition
- Recommended master size: 1440 x 3200 pixels
- 9:20 portrait wallpaper ratio, suitable for modern phones
- High-resolution, clean enough for app UI overlays and export/share cards

Product context:
This background will be used inside Stunity, an e-learning plus social media app. Students may select their own photo to place on top of the background. The app may also use this background for monthly honor roll, quiz leaderboard, learning streaks, profile performance, badges, and other achievement surfaces.

Creative direction:
Design a beautiful, modern, motivational academic wallpaper with Cambodian elegance. It should feel premium, aspirational, calm, and youthful. The background must support future dynamic UI overlays such as a student profile photo, rank badge, score cards, monthly leaderboard details, quiz performance, streaks, and social profile stats.

Use a true white background with soft pearl-grey relief, not yellow or cream. Keep the center clean and luminous. Use subtle Modern Khmer Excellence elements: white sculpted paper layers, faint Khmer landmark relief near the lower third, elegant kbach-inspired cloud relief, thin gold guide lines, tiny constellation points, burgundy and teal corner accents with gold trim.

Composition:
- Reserve the top 12% for future app header/status area and optional school or Stunity label.
- Reserve the upper-middle 24% for a future student profile photo or hero portrait.
- Reserve the central 30% for dynamic achievement cards, rank, score, badges, leaderboard status, or monthly performance.
- Reserve the bottom 18% for profile actions, tabs, social stats, or app navigation.
- Keep all important decorative elements away from the center vertical content lane.
- Create a strong but subtle visual frame along corners and lower edges.

Visual structure:
- Top corners: small burgundy and teal folded accents with fine gold trim, very restrained.
- Side margins: delicate gold academic guide lines, constellation dots, and circular geometry.
- Lower third: soft white relief of Angkor Wat, Bayon, Banteay Srei, or Preah Vihear, blended into layered white paper waves.
- Bottom area: premium sculpted white waves and faint Khmer cloud/kbach relief that can sit behind app navigation without reducing readability.
- Background field: clean true white, slightly luminous, with very subtle cool shadows and texture.

App readability:
- Leave a clean central vertical corridor for profile photo and leaderboard UI.
- Avoid placing dark or detailed graphics where future text/cards will appear.
- Make the background beautiful even when blurred slightly behind UI cards.
- It should work in both profile screen and shareable achievement story/card contexts.

Mood:
Fresh, aspirational, proud, Cambodian, modern, learning-focused, social-app friendly, premium but not formal-heavy.

Avoid:
No text, no numbers, no logos, no school names, no app UI mockup, no phone frame, no people, no student face, no photo frame, no badges, no medals, no trophies, no certificate, no leaderboard text, no yellow/cream-heavy background, no dark center, no clutter behind future profile or performance content.
```
