# Poster Studio Design Collection 2 Heritage Khmer Prompts

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This file keeps the original Khmer heritage direction separate from the newer modern-white direction. Use it when the poster should feel more Cambodian, ceremonial, and culturally decorative, with subtle kbach ornament, landmark watermarks, laurel details, and premium burgundy-gold-teal academic styling.

Do not copy any school logo, seal, partner badge, exact layout, text, or brand identity from the reference images. Use references only for readability, hierarchy, and academic-poster discipline.

## Shared Direction

- Visual identity: premium Cambodian academic excellence, clean, ceremonial, professional, readable.
- Base color: white or very light ivory-white. Keep the layout bright and spacious.
- Accent palette: deep burgundy `#6B132B`, champagne gold `#D8B35F`, deep academic teal `#0E5552`.
- Cambodian detail: subtle Khmer kbach-inspired curves, ornamental corner linework, cultural texture, and landmark watermarks.
- Landmark style: Angkor Wat, Bayon, Bakong, Banteay Srei, or Preah Vihear as refined low-contrast line-art, never photorealistic.
- Avoid: text, letters, numbers, logos, copied school emblems, students, faces, watermarks, signatures, heavy confetti, crowded student area.

## 1. Square Background - Heritage Khmer

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: square congratulation poster background for a web poster generator
Primary request: Create an original premium square congratulation poster background for a modern Cambodian school with elegant Khmer heritage styling.

Canvas:
- Exact 1:1 square composition
- 2160 x 2160 pixels
- High-resolution professional print and social-media quality

Creative direction:
Design a clean, sophisticated academic achievement background inspired by Cambodian cultural elegance. The artwork must be original and must not copy any existing school poster, logo, layout, seal, or brand identity.

Use a bright white or very light ivory-white base covering approximately 75-80% of the canvas. Add subtle pearl paper texture, soft light gradients, faint geometric dot patterns, translucent layered curves, and restrained Khmer kbach-inspired ornamental linework.

Create an elegant Stunity visual identity using three flowing accent movements:
- deep burgundy #6B132B
- champagne gold #D8B35F
- deep academic teal #0E5552

The accents should feel smooth, luxurious, dimensional, and modern, with soft highlights and realistic depth. Place the strongest decorative movement around the lower corners and partially along one upper corner. Keep the middle calm and spacious.

Add a few restrained academic and Khmer heritage details near the edges only:
- abstract graduation-cap line icon
- tiny gold confetti particles
- subtle laurel fragments
- fine circular geometry
- very subtle Khmer kbach-inspired curves

Landmark watermark:
Add a light illustrated Cambodian landmark watermark near the lower central background, such as Angkor Wat, Bayon, Bakong, Banteay Srei, or Preah Vihear. Render it as fine line-art with very low contrast and soft pearl-gold or pale teal hints. It must support the poster atmosphere without reducing readability.

Composition and safe zones:
- Keep the upper central 18% visually quiet for school logo, school name, and poster title
- Keep the central 58% clean for flexible student layouts: Top 3, Top 5, or Top 10
- Keep the bottom 12% calm for footer information
- Decorative graphics must stay near the edges and must never dominate the student area
- Ensure strong visual balance even when viewed as a small social-media thumbnail

Lighting:
Soft studio lighting, elegant shadows, subtle metallic-gold highlights, premium editorial finish.

Avoid:
No text, no Khmer or English lettering, no numbers, no logos, no school emblems, no students, no faces, no photo frames, no signatures, no certificates, no large graduation objects, no heavy central decoration.
```
